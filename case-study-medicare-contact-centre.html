The Challenge

A large Medicare Advantage enrollment BPO ran a competitive partner selection after a previous vendor left an underperforming Amazon Connect implementation. Armakuni won on an assessment-first approach — providing the first honest picture of the platform before writing any code.

Scale: 600 agents. Multi-tenant BPO serving multiple Medicare Advantage health plan clients. Ranked among Illinois' top 5 billers for SMBs.

A four-week assessment grew into a 12-month Connect Development POD, which expanded to include a dedicated Outbound Phase engagement.

What We Built
Engagement Scope
Platform Assessment SOW Fixed scope. Current-state report, risk plan, architecture diagram, configuration workbook, execution plan.
Connect Development POD 12-month retainer, 100 hrs/month. CI/CD pipelines, PR workflows, PHI-compliant deployment model.
Outbound Phase POD 3-month engagement, 200 hrs/month. Stateful orchestration layer with deterministic lifecycle management.
Outcomes
1
Assessment-first approach won a competitive partner selection

Armakuni gave leadership a clear, honest picture of what was broken and a sequenced plan to fix it — before any code was written or budget committed.

2
First honest baseline unlocked the right investment and structure

Before the assessment, the BPO had no reliable picture of its own platform. The four-week engagement delivered five artefacts that validated pod sizing on evidence.

3
600 agents operating with improved stability and release governance

Frequent bugs, uncontrolled releases, and missing platform features are being addressed systematically. CI/CD, PR workflows, and a PHI-compliant deployment model are in place for the first time.

4
Supervisors now act on problems before customers feel them

Real-time dashboards, Contact Lens in-call analytics, and structured CloudWatch monitoring. Medicare disclaimer compliance monitoring is now automated.

5
Outbound now runs on a stateful orchestration layer

Manual recycling, fragile campaign progression, and disposition-based reporting replaced. Policy enforcement, automated outcomes, and lifecycle-based metrics.

6
Engagement expanded across three SOWs as each phase delivered

Started with a four-week, $5,000 assessment. That earned a 12-month POD. The POD earned the Outbound Phase. Land-and-expand as a direct result of the assessment-first model.
